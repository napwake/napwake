## Status conditions

Each has a `Reason`, a `Message` and a `LastTransitionTime`. It appears once it first applies, then shows `False` with a False reason below, rather than going. Only `GitOpsConflict`, `Autoscaled` and `PausingOff` are removed.

| Type | `True` means | Reasons |
|------|--------------|---------|
| <Ref id="condition-targetavailable">TargetAvailable</Ref> | The target exists, and is managed | `TargetExists`. False: `TargetNotFound`, `TargetIgnored` (labelled `napwake.dev/ignore`) |
| <Ref id="condition-metricsavailable">MetricsAvailable</Ref> | CPU can be measured, for the activity clock and forecast | `MetricsReported`. Unknown: `NoReadyPods`, `AwaitingPodMetrics`, while new pods have no metrics. False: `NoPodMetrics`, `NoCPURequests`, `MetricsUnavailable` |
| <Ref id="condition-prometheusavailable">PrometheusAvailable</Ref> | The activity queries answer. Only set when there are some | `QueriesEvaluated`. False: `QueryFailed`, `EndpointNotConfigured`, `SeriesLost` (a query that matched series at the last check matches none, as after Prometheus restarts without its data) |
| <Ref id="condition-prometheusqueryempty">PrometheusQueryEmpty</Ref> | A query has matched no series since first evaluated. A metric or label may be misspelt. Or nothing has made the series yet, as with a request counter before the first request. It doesn't stop a pause | `NoSeries`. False: `QueriesMatchSeries` |
| <Ref id="condition-idlevetoed">IdleVetoed</Ref> | Idle, but a confident forecast expects demand in the coming hour, which the message names. The pause waits | `ForecastExpectsDemand`. False: `NotVetoed` |
| <Ref id="condition-wakeonrequest">WakeOnRequest</Ref> | Its Services are routed to the doorman while paused, so a request wakes it, or with [`wake.mode: Ask`](/concepts/wake-on-request/#asking-before-waking) a person's click. With `Ask`, its message says the doorman asks a person's browser first, and turns away other requests. A Services problem stays once it's awake, the message starting `at its last pause:` | `DoormanRouted`. False: [why it isn't routed](/concepts/wake-on-request/#when-a-workload-isnt-routed) (`NoServices`, `ServedByOtherPods`, `UnsupportedIPFamily`, `UnsupportedLoadBalancer`, `ForwarderNotReady`, `DoormanUnavailable`, `NoFreePort`, `TooManyPorts`, `RoutingFailed`, `DoormanDisabled`, `NodeLocalTraffic`). `NotPaused`, `TargetNotFound`, `DuplicateTarget` once routing is removed |
| <Ref id="condition-heldbydependents">HeldByDependents</Ref> | Kept awake for its dependents | `DependentsAwake`. False: `NoAwakeDependents`, or `NotIdle` once its own activity restarts its clock |
| <Ref id="condition-waitingfordependencies">WaitingForDependencies</Ref> | Its resume waits for a `waitForReady` dependency to be Ready | `DependencyNotReady`. False: `DependenciesReady`. Also `DependencyWontStart`, naming a dependency it doesn't wait for, as it can't start or wasn't Ready in time. Or `DependencyCycle`, naming one that waits for it in turn |
| <Ref id="condition-dependencycycle">DependencyCycle</Ref> | `dependsOn` forms a cycle through it, so it won't pause | `DependencyCycle`. False: `NoCycle` |
| <Ref id="condition-dependencynotfound">DependencyNotFound</Ref> | A `dependsOn` workload doesn't exist, or can't be seen | `DependencyNotFound`, or `DependencyNotVisible` when it's only outside `watchNamespaces` or not readable. False: `DependenciesFound`, `NoDependencies` |
| <Ref id="condition-dependencynotallowed">DependencyNotAllowed</Ref> | A dependency, declared or learned, is in a namespace whose `napwake.dev/dependents-from` doesn't list this one. It isn't held, woken or waited for | `DependencyNotAllowed`. False: `DependenciesAllowed`, `NoDependencies` |
| <Ref id="condition-duplicatetarget">DuplicateTarget</Ref> | An older ManagedWorkload manages the target. This one hands back any pause it made, then does nothing | `DuplicateTarget`. False: `Resolved` |
| <Ref id="condition-protected">Protected</Ref> | It's never paused. It's in a protected namespace, Napwake's own operator or doorman, or a StatefulSet that deletes its PVCs when scaled down | `ProtectedNamespace`, `NapwakeComponent`, `PVCsDeletedOnScaleDown`. False: `NotProtected` |
| <Ref id="condition-pauserequest">PauseRequest</Ref> | The last [pause request](/guides/pause/#pause-now) paused it, or found it paused. False says why not. Until the next request, it says what happened and when, such as `paused as requested at 2026-10-21 03:32 UTC` | `Paused`, `AlreadyPaused`. False: `DryRun`, `PausingOff` (pausing is off for its install), `ForecastExpectsDemand`, `ActiveUntil`, `HeldByDependents`, `DependencyCycle`, `Protected`, `ScaledToZero`, `TargetNotFound`, `TargetIgnored`, `DuplicateTarget`, `PredatesStatus`, `PauseCancelled` (a request came as it paused, or a dependent was awake) |
| <Ref id="condition-wakerequest">WakeRequest</Ref> | The last [wake request](/guides/pause/#wake-now) woke it, or found it awake. False says why not. Until the next request, it says what happened and when, such as `woken as requested at 2026-10-21 03:32 UTC` | `Woken`, `AlreadyAwake`. False: `TargetNotFound`, `TargetIgnored`, `DuplicateTarget`, `Protected`, `ScaledToZero`, `BeingDeleted`, `PredatesStatus` |
| <Ref id="condition-gitopsconflict">GitOpsConflict</Ref> | A [GitOps tool](/guides/gitops/) undid its last pause. Removed once a pause holds for an hour | `PauseUndone` |
| <Ref id="condition-resumeincomplete">ResumeIncomplete</Ref> | The last resume stopped waiting with replicas not Ready. It runs with the Ready ones until all are ([troubleshooting](/operations/troubleshooting/#workload-resumed-with-replicas-not-ready)) | `ReplicasNotReady`. False: `AllReplicasReady` |
| <Ref id="condition-pauseincomplete">PauseIncomplete</Ref> | The last pause stopped waiting for pods still running a minute past their termination grace period. It's `Paused` regardless ([troubleshooting](/operations/troubleshooting/#workload-paused-with-pods-still-running)) | `PodsStillRunning`. False: `PodsStopped`, `NotPaused` |
| <Ref id="condition-pausedwhileinuse">PausedWhileInUse</Ref> | Its own use undid its last pause soon after it began. So its idle clock waits longer, until a pause holds. The message says what to change ([Paused while in use](/concepts/idle-detection/#paused-while-in-use)) | `WokenSoonAfterPause`, `PauseCancelled`. False: `PauseHeld`. Also `DryRun`, `NoIdlePolicy`, `Protected`, `TargetIgnored` or `TargetNotFound`, once no pause can hold. Pausing off keeps it |
| <Ref id="condition-pausingoff">PausingOff</Ref> | `True` on every ManagedWorkload of an install with `pausing.enabled: false` ([Stop all pausing now](/guides/pause/#stop-all-pausing-now)). Removed once pausing is on again | `DisabledForTheInstall` |
| <Ref id="condition-autoscaled">Autoscaled</Ref> | An HPA or KEDA ScaledObject scales it, with its range. Removed when neither does ([HPA and KEDA](/guides/autoscalers/)) | `HPA`, `KEDA` |
| <Ref id="condition-scaledtozero">ScaledToZero</Ref> | Scaled to zero outside Napwake, so left off. Not paused, woken or routed until its replicas are above zero | `ScaledToZero`. False: `HasReplicas` |

## Events

Each user-visible change emits one event, as it happens, shown by `kubectl describe`. The opt-in controller puts <Ref id="workload-event-managed">Managed</Ref>, <Ref id="workload-event-nolongermanaged">NoLongerManaged</Ref>, <Ref id="workload-event-invalidsetting">InvalidSetting</Ref>, <Ref id="workload-event-labelignored">LabelIgnored</Ref> and <Ref id="workload-event-protected">Protected</Ref> on the Deployment or StatefulSet itself. In dry-run, events for decisions it holds back start with `[dry-run]` ([Dry run](/guides/dry-run/#events)).

Every event on the ManagedWorkload, by what it's about.

| About | Events |
|-------|--------|
| The lifecycle | <Ref id="event-idledetected">IdleDetected</Ref>, <Ref id="event-pausedearly">PausedEarly</Ref> (brought forward by [`pauseEarly`](/concepts/forecasting/#pausing-early)), <Ref id="event-pauserequested">PauseRequested</Ref>, <Ref id="event-paused">Paused</Ref>, <Ref id="event-resumed">Resumed</Ref>, <Ref id="event-activityresumed">ActivityResumed</Ref> (in dry-run, with what the pause would have freed), <Ref id="event-unobserved">Unobserved</Ref> (a [gap in watching](/concepts/idle-detection/#restarts-and-outages) restarted the idle clock, and how long it was) |
| Its pause record lost from status | <Ref id="event-pauserecovered">PauseRecovered</Ref> (from the target's `napwake.dev/paused-from`). The warning <Ref id="event-pauserecordlost">PauseRecordLost</Ref> (nothing to finish it from) |
| A pause request not acted on | Its reason says why, as the `PauseRequest` condition's does |
| A wake request not acted on | Its reason says why, as the `WakeRequest` condition's does |
| What woke it | <Ref id="event-wokebyactivity">WokeByActivity</Ref>, <Ref id="event-wokenbyrequest">WokenByRequest</Ref> (from the doorman, saying a person asked when it's a click on **Wake it**), <Ref id="event-wakerequested">WakeRequested</Ref> (from `napwake.dev/wake-requested`, as `kubectl napwake wake` sets it), <Ref id="event-autoresume">AutoResume</Ref>, <Ref id="event-scaledup">ScaledUp</Ref>, <Ref id="event-dryrunwake">DryRunWake</Ref>, <Ref id="event-pausingoffwake">PausingOffWake</Ref> (pausing [turned off](/guides/pause/#stop-all-pausing-now) for its install), and <Ref id="event-pausecancelled">PauseCancelled</Ref> (whatever would wake it came as it paused, or a dependent was awake just before it was scaled to zero, so the pause was [called off](/guides/pause/#a-request-calls-the-pause-off)) |
| A pause made while it was in use | The warning <Ref id="event-pausedwhileinuse">PausedWhileInUse</Ref>, with what to change ([Paused while in use](/concepts/idle-detection/#paused-while-in-use)) |
| Left off, scaled to zero outside Napwake | <Ref id="event-scaledtozero">ScaledToZero</Ref> |
| What holds it awake | <Ref id="event-idlevetoed">IdleVetoed</Ref>, <Ref id="event-heldbydependents">HeldByDependents</Ref>, <Ref id="event-dependencycycle">DependencyCycle</Ref> |
| A GitOps tool undoing a pause | <Ref id="event-gitopsconflict">GitOpsConflict</Ref>, and <Ref id="event-gitopsconflictresolved">GitOpsConflictResolved</Ref> when that ends |
| Dependencies | The warnings <Ref id="event-dependencywontstart">DependencyWontStart</Ref> and `DependencyCycle` (a resume not waiting for a `waitForReady` dependency) and <Ref id="event-dependencywakefailed">DependencyWakeFailed</Ref> (one couldn't be woken). <Ref id="event-dependencieslearned">DependenciesLearned</Ref>, for learned ones |
| The forecast | <Ref id="event-predictionfed">PredictionFed</Ref> each hour it learns, <Ref id="event-regimechange">RegimeChange</Ref> when demoted, <Ref id="event-forecastreset">ForecastReset</Ref> when its saved state can't be read. When Napwake begins managing a workload, <Ref id="event-forecastlearnedhistory">ForecastLearnedHistory</Ref> if it learned the CPU history from Prometheus. <Ref id="event-forecastlearninglive">ForecastLearningLive</Ref> if there was none, or the warning <Ref id="event-forecasthistoryunavailable">ForecastHistoryUnavailable</Ref> if it couldn't be read |
| The GKE NEG [forwarder](/concepts/wake-on-request/#gke-container-native-load-balancing-negs) | <Ref id="event-forwarderstarted">ForwarderStarted</Ref> beside a paused workload, <Ref id="event-forwarderstopped">ForwarderStopped</Ref> once it's removed after the wake |
| Problems | <Ref id="event-targetnotfound">TargetNotFound</Ref>, <Ref id="event-duplicatetarget">DuplicateTarget</Ref>, <Ref id="event-nopodmetrics">NoPodMetrics</Ref>, <Ref id="event-nocpurequests">NoCPURequests</Ref>, <Ref id="event-metricsunavailable">MetricsUnavailable</Ref>, <Ref id="event-queryfailed">QueryFailed</Ref>, <Ref id="event-endpointnotconfigured">EndpointNotConfigured</Ref>, <Ref id="event-prometheusqueryempty">PrometheusQueryEmpty</Ref>, <Ref id="event-invalidactivityannotation">InvalidActivityAnnotation</Ref>, <Ref id="event-routingfailed">RoutingFailed</Ref>, <Ref id="event-unsupportedloadbalancer">UnsupportedLoadBalancer</Ref>, <Ref id="event-requestnotserved">RequestNotServed</Ref>, <Ref id="event-protected">Protected</Ref>, and these: |

| Problem | Means |
|---------|-------|
| <Ref id="event-noreadinessprobe">NoReadinessProbe</Ref> | A routed workload's pods have no readiness probe. A waking request may then reach the app before it can answer ([Readiness probes](/concepts/wake-on-request/#readiness-probes)) |
| <Ref id="event-resumeincomplete">ResumeIncomplete</Ref> | In place of `Resumed`, when the resume stopped waiting for replicas not Ready. <Ref id="event-allreplicasready">AllReplicasReady</Ref> once they are |
| <Ref id="event-pauseincomplete">PauseIncomplete</Ref> | In place of `Paused`, when the pause stopped waiting for pods that didn't stop |
| <Ref id="event-handbackabandoned">HandBackAbandoned</Ref> | A ManagedWorkload in a namespace being deleted let go of its workload |
| <Ref id="event-managedbyanotherinstall">ManagedByAnotherInstall</Ref> | This install leaves the ManagedWorkload to the [one that took it on](/getting-started/installation/#more-than-one-install) |
